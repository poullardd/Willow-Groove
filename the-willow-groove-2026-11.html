The 15-Second Huddle

A Note from Matt

We’ve come through the heaviest stretch of the year, and you carried it. Weddings back to back, a full dining room most nights, a spa book with no quiet week since August. The fall surveys are full of your names.

November is lighter, but not light — Thanksgiving, then the holiday parties right behind it. Take the quieter mornings where you get them. Thank you for this season.

The Handoff
